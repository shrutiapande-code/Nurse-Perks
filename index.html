How to get the discount

  1. Create a free account with ID.me and/or SheerID and complete nurse or healthcare-worker verification.
  2. At checkout, look for “Verify with ID.me,” “Healthcare,” “Medical professional,” or “First responder” discount links.
  3. Compare the discounted price with sale prices, shipping, and membership costs. A percentage off is not always the lowest final price.
  4. For in-person offers, ask before paying and bring your work ID or professional credentials. Local participation can vary.

Who may qualify? ID.me’s U.S. nurse eligibility page includes RNs, LPNs/LVNs, NPs, CRNAs, CNAs, nurse aides, PCTs, public-health nurses, and other listed roles. Student nurses and nursing students may have separate student offers, but should not assume they qualify for nurse-only programs.
